# The 30-second demo — ScreenToGif script (the one thing Claude can't record)

Record at 1280×720-ish, ~12fps. Ten beats, ~3s each:
1. Open the live site — paste (Ctrl+V) a screenshot of any app.
2. Press S, drag a box around a broken area — it becomes a piece; speak a note via 🎤.
3. Press A, draw an arrow; type its note; pick a severity from the new dropdowns.
4. Drop a mockulog-….txt onto the board — watch it attach under Items.
5. Click **Export Package** — show the zip landing.
6. Open report.md for two seconds — the geometry, tags, and Context block.
7. Click **Enter Wireframe Mode** — the wordmark flips to MockuFRAME.
8. Click **Dashboard** preset; marquee the three cards; hit ⇤ then ⋯.
9. Click **👁 Preview** — desktop/tablet/phone, hold two seconds.
10. End card: the README tagline. Save as demo.gif, drop it at the top of the README.
